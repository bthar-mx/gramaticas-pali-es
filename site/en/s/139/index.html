139. 245. Tumh’-āmhākaṃ tayi mayi (318)

[Tumha-amhākaṃ + tayi-mayi = 2 words]

Sabbessaṃ tumha-amhasaddānaṃ savibhattīnaṃ tayi mayi icc ete ādesā honti yathāsaṅkhyaṃ smiṃmhi vibhattimhi.

Tayi, mayi.

Smiṃmhī ti kimatthaṃ? Tvaṃ bhavasi. Ahaṃ bhavāmi.

“Tayi” and “mayi” [of] “tumha” and “amha”.
When the inflection ‘smiṃ’ follows, the whole word “tumha” together with the inflection is substituted by “tayi”, and the whole word “amha” together with the inflection is substituted by “mayi”.
Examples
  1. Tayi = tumha + smiṃ (“tumha” together with the inflection ‘smiṃ’ is substituted by “tayi” (§139)).
  2. Mayi = amha + smiṃ (“amha” together with the inflection ‘smiṃ’ is substituted by “mayi” (§139)).

What is the purpose of saying “when the inflection ‘smiṃ’ follows”? [This sutta does not apply when ‘smiṃ’ does not follow, such as in] tvaṃ bhavasi, ahaṃ bhavāmi.
