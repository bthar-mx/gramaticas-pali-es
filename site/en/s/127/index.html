§127

To-ti-tā sa-smiṃ-nāsu

When ‘sa’, ‘smiṃ’ and ‘nā’ follow, [there are] ‘to’, ‘ti’ and ‘tā’.

127. 102. To-ti-tā sa-smiṃ-nāsu (303)

[To-ti-tā + sa-smiṃ-nāsu = 2 words]

Sabbass’ eva ntupaccayassa savibhattissa to-ti-tādesā honti vā sa smiṃ nā icc etesu yathāsaṅkhyaṃ.

Guṇavato, guṇavantassa; guṇavati, guṇavantasmiṃ; guṇavatā, guṇavantena; satimato, satimantassa; satimati, satimantasmiṃ; satimatā, satimantena.

Etesv iti kimatthaṃ? Guṇavā. Satimā (M. i, 70).

When ‘sa’, ‘smiṃ’ and ‘nā’ follow, [there are] ‘to’, ‘ti’ and ‘tā’.
Optionally, when ‘sa’, ‘smiṃ’ and ‘nā’ follow, the whole suffix ‘ntu’ together with the inflection is substituted by ‘to’, ‘ti’ and ‘tā’ respectively.
Example
  1. Guṇavato = guṇavantu + sa (‘ntu’ together with the inflection ‘sa’ is substituted by ‘to’ (§127)).
  2. Guṇavati = guṇavantu + smiṃ (‘ntu’ together with the inflection ‘smiṃ’ is substituted by ‘ti’ (§127)).
  3. Guṇavatā = guṇavantu + nā (‘ntu’ together with the inflection ‘nā’ is substituted by ‘tā’ (§127)).
  4. Satimato = satimantu + sa (‘ntu’ together with the inflection ‘sa’ is substituted by ‘to’ (§127)).
  5. Satimati = satimantu + smiṃ (‘ntu’ together with the inflection ‘smiṃ’ is substituted by ‘ti’ (§127)).
  6. Satimatā = satimantu + nā (‘ntu’ together with the inflection ‘nā’ is substituted by ‘tā’ (§127)).

What is the purpose of saying “when these follow”? [This sutta does not apply when these do not follow, such as in] guṇavā, satimā.
