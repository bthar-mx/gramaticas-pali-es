457. 424. Bhūvādayo dhātavo (938)

[Bhū-ādayo + dhātavo = 2 words]

Bhū icc evamādayo ye saddagaṇā, te dhātusaññā honti.

Bhavati (M. ii, 338), bhavanti (M. ii, 335), carati, caranti (M. i, 129), pacati, pacanti, cintayati, cintayanti, hoti (M. i, 10), honti (M. i, 10), gacchati (M. iii, 338), gacchanti (A. ii, 5).

“Bhū” and others are roots.
Those groups of words that begin with “bhū” are called “dhātu” (root).
Examples
  1. Bhavati, bhavanti ([he] is, [they] are).
  2. Carati, caranti ([he] walks, [they] walk).
  3. Pacati, pacanti ([he] cooks, [they] cook).
  4. Cintayati, cintayanti ([he] thinks, [they] think).
  5. Hoti, honti ([he] is, [they] are).
  6. Gacchati, gacchanti ([he] goes, [they] go).
Thus [ends] the second section in the verb chapter
