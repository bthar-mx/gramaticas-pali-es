28. 40. Para dvebhāvo ṭhāne (67)

[Para + dve-bhāvo + ṭhāne = 3 words]

Saramhā parassa byañjanassa dvebhāvo (doubling) hoti ṭhāne (in the appropriate place).

Idha ppamādo, purisassa jantuno, pabbajjaṃ kittayissāmi (Khu. i, 340), cātuddasiṃ (A. i, 142), pañcaddasiṃ (A. i, 142), abhikkantataro cando.

Ṭhāne ti kasmā? Idha modati pecca modati (Khu. i, 15).

Doubling of the following in the appropriate place.
After a vowel, there is doubling of the following consonant, in the appropriate place.
Examples: “Idha ppamādo, purisassa jantuno”, “pabbajjaṃ kittayissāmi”, “cātuddasiṃ”, “pañcaddasiṃ”, “abhikkantataro cando”.
