344Kaccāyana Sutta. 361Rūpasiddhi Sutta.Vā ṇ’ apacce (752)Saddanīti-Suttamālā Sutta
[Vā + ṇa + apacce = 3 words]

Ṇapaccayo hoti vā “tass’ āpaccam” icc etasmiṃ atthe.

Vasiṭṭhassa apaccaṃ Vāsiṭṭho (Vin. ii, 11; D. i, 225; iii, 66)Vinaya-piṭaka · Dīgha-nikāya, Vasiṭṭhassa apaccaṃ vā. Vasiṭṭhassa apaccaṃ Vāsiṭṭhī. Vasiṭṭhassa apaccaṃ Vāsiṭṭhaṃ. Evaṃ Bhāradvājo (D. i, 225; M. i, 47; A. ii, 197)Dīgha-nikāya · Majjhima-nikāya · Aṅguttara-nikāya, Bhāradvājī, Bhāradvājaṃ. Gotamo (Vin. i, 1)Vinaya-piṭaka · vol. i, p. 1, Gotamī (M. iii, 296; a. iii, 101)Majjhima-nikāya, Gotamaṃ (D. i, 236)Dīgha-nikāya · vol. i, p. 236. Vāsudevo (JA. iv, 81)Jātaka-aṭṭhakathā · vol. iv, p. 81, Vāsudevī, Vāsudevaṃ. Bāladevo (JA. iv, 81)Jātaka-aṭṭhakathā · vol. iv, p. 81, Bāladevī, Bāladevaṃ. Vesāmitto (D. i, 97; A. ii, 197)Dīgha-nikāya · Aṅguttara-nikāya, Vesamittī, Vesāmittaṃ.

Optionally, [there is] ‘ṇa’ in [the meaning of] offspring.
Optionally, there is the suffix ‘ṇa’ in this meaning: “the offspring of him (tass’ āpaccaṃ)”.
Examples
  1. Vasiṭṭhassa apaccaṃ (the offspring of Vasiṭṭha): Vāsiṭṭho, Vāsiṭṭhī, Vāsiṭṭhaṃ.

Likewise:

  1. Bharadvājassa apaccaṃ (the offspring of Bharadvāja): Bhāradvājo, Bhāradvājī, Bhāradvājaṃ.
  2. Gotamassa apaccaṃ (the offspring of Gotama): Gotamo, Gotamī, Gotamaṃ.
  3. Vasudevassa apaccaṃ (the offspring of Vasudeva): Vāsudevo, Vāsudevī, Vāsudevaṃ.
  4. Baladevassa apaccaṃ (the offspring of Baladeva): Bāladevo, Bāladevī, Bāladevaṃ.
  5. Visāmittassa apaccaṃ (the offspring of Visāmitta): Vesāmitto, Vesamittī, Vesāmittaṃ.

350Kaccāyana Sutta. 373Rūpasiddhi Sutta.Yena vā saṃsaṭṭhaṃ tarati carati vahati ṇiko (764)Saddanīti-Suttamālā Sutta
[Yena + vā + saṃsaṭṭhaṃ + tarati + carati + vahati + ṇiko = 7 words]

Yena vā saṃsaṭṭhaṃ, yena vā tarati, yena vā carati, yena vā vahati icc etesv atthesu ṇikapaccayo hoti vā.

Tilena saṃsaṭṭhaṃ bhojanaṃ telikaṃ, tilena saṃsaṭṭhaṃ vā. Evaṃ goḷikaṃ, ghātikaṃ.

Nāvāya taratī ti nāviko (Khu. vi, 79)Khuddaka-nikāya · vol. vi, p. 79, nāvāya tarati vā. Evaṃ oḷumpiko.

Sakaṭena caratī ti sākaṭiko (S. i, 56)Saṃyutta-nikāya · vol. i, p. 56, sakaṭena carati vā. Evaṃ pattiko (D. i, 47)Dīgha-nikāya · vol. i, p. 47, daṇḍiko, dhammiko (D. i, 80; M. ii, 262; Khu. ii, 273)Dīgha-nikāya · Majjhima-nikāya · Khuddaka-nikāya, pādiko.

Sīsena vahatī ti sīsiko, sīsena vahati vā. Aṃsena vahatī ti aṃsiko, aṃsena vahati vā. Evaṃ khandhiko, aṅguliko.

Vā ti vikappanatthena aññesu pi ṇikapaccayo hoti. Rājagahe vasatī ti rājagahiko, rājagahe vasati vā. Rājagahe jāto rājagahiko, rājagahe jāto vā. Evaṃ māgadhiko (M. i, 287)Majjhima-nikāya · vol. i, p. 287, sāvatthiko, kapilavatthiko, pāṭaliputtiko, vesāliko (Vin. i, 27; A. iii, 47)Vinaya-piṭaka · Aṅguttara-nikāya.

[There is] ‘ṇika’ [in these meanings]: mixed with that, he crosses by that, he goes by that, he carries by that.
Optionally, there is the suffix ‘ṇika’ in these meanings: “mixed with that (yena saṃsaṭṭhaṃ)”, “he crosses by that (yena tarati)”, “he goes by that (yena carati)”, “he carries by that (yena vahati)”.
Examples
  1. Tilena saṃsaṭṭhaṃ bhojanaṃ: telikaṃ (food mixed with sesamum seeds).
