## Notes

1. Dates are approximate (c. = circa; ? = doubtful). Where sources disagree, the main alternatives are given. Dates and attributions follow Bode, the Dictionary of Pāli Proper Names, von Hinüber, Pind, Ruiz-Falqués and Ñāṇatusita.
2. **Nyāsappadīpa and Saṃpyaṅ-ṭīkā.** The earlier version of this chart treated the Nyāsappadīpa, the Saṃpyaṅ-ṭīkā and the Thanbyin-ṭīkā as one work. Ñāṇatusita lists the Nyāsappadīpa (Thanbyin, commissioned by Narapatisithu) under 5.1.11 and a separate «Mukhamattadīpanī-purāṇaṭīkā, Saṃpyaṅ-ṭīkā» under 5.1.112. The question is open; the two rows are kept separate here until the printed editions (1913, 1925) are compared.
3. **Saddabindu.** Ñāṇatusita, following Lottermoser (JPTS XI, 1987), ascribes it to Rājaguru Nārada (1481) and states explicitly that it is «not by King Kyaswa». The traditional ascription to Kyaswa is kept as an alternative.
4. **Kaccāyanasāra and its ṭīkās.** Ñāṇatusita dates the Kaccāyanasāra to the 14th–15th c. but its two ṭīkās by Siri Saddhammavilāsa to the 13th c., which cannot both be right. The dates of the ṭīkās are given as he has them; they need checking.
5. GRETIL links were verified against a snapshot of GRETIL's file tree in October 2026. GRETIL is migrating to the TextGrid repository; if a link fails, the same path exists in the INDOLOGY/GRETIL-mirror repository on GitHub, and the whole Pāli corpus is at https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/2_pali.zip.
6. «No online text located» means no free e-text or scan was found in the earlier search. «Not checked» marks rows added in this revision, for which no search was made. Burmese printed editions may be digitized under Burmese-script titles.

## Changes in this revision (from Ñāṇatusita)

- **Corrected:** «Saddhammanāsinī» is not a separate ṭīkā on Kaccāyana: Ñāṇatusita gives it as another name of the Sammohavināsinī, the new ṭīkā on the Kaccāyanasāra (row merged). Dhātumañjūsā: Sri Lanka, 14th c. (was «date uncertain»). Kaccāyanavaṇṇanā: 1626/1627. Sāratthavikāsinī: Ariyālaṅkāra II, Sagaing, 1606. Niruttisāramañjūsā: Sagaing, mid 17th c.
- **Alternatives added:** Nyāsa date (11th–12th c.); Saddabindu author and date; Vibhattyattha author; Abhinavacūḷanirutti author; Suttaniddesa coded as Sri Lankan; Nyāsappadīpa commissioned by Narapatisithu.
- **Rows added:** Atthavyākhyāna; Kaccāyananissaya; Mukhamattadīpanī-purāṇaṭīkā; Mukhamattasāraṭīkā; Rūpasiddhi-sannaya and Sandehavighātinī; Kaccāyanarūpadīpanī; Bālāvatāra ṭīkās (Uttama; Subodhikā); Kaccāyanabheda-mahāṭīkā (author: Uttamasikkha, 1669, so no longer «unknown»); Kaccāyanasāraṭīkā and purāṇaṭīkā; Kārikāṭīkā; Etimāsamidīpikā; separate rows for the Saddatthabhedacintā, Sambandhacintā and Saddabindu ṭīkās; Mahānirutti, Niruttipiṭaka, Niruttimañjūsā; Kaccāyanavaṇṇanācakkakyan; the Visuddhāyuṃ (2004).
- **New column «Ñ»** with Ñāṇatusita's section number, so each row can be checked.

## Completeness: known omissions and open questions

This chart covers the main works of the Kaccāyana school but is not a complete inventory.

**Still to add or check.** The Kaccāyanatthadīpanī, a Thai commentary on the Mūlakaccāyana, was not found in the pages of Ñāṇatusita read for this revision; it belongs in section D once its source is confirmed. Ñāṇatusita also lists many minor titles under 5.1 that are only names (e.g. Kaccāyana-aṭṭhakathā, Kaccāyana-atthavinicchaya, Pabhedapakaraṇa, Suttāvali, Padamālā, Kaccāyanasandhivisodhanī-ṭīkā, Sammohavighātanī on the Sandhikappa); they are left out unless more is known about them.

**Works that may belong, depending on where the boundary of the school is drawn.** Ñāṇatusita groups a number of minor Burmese treatises together with the Kaccāyana digests (his 5.4, «14 minor texts»): the Saddavutti of Saddhammapāla with its ṭīkās and the Saddavuttivivaraṇa; the Sambandhamālinī of Saddhammālaṅkāra (before 1442); the Vācavācaka of Dhammadassī (14th c.) and its ṭīkās; the Gandhābhāraṇa of Ariyavaṃsa (1436–7); the Gandhaṭṭhi; the Bālappabodhana; the Niruttibheda; and short treatises on letters (Akkharakosalla, Akkharabheda, Akkharavisodhanī). They could be added once it is confirmed that each is built on Kaccāyana. The Liṅgatthavivaraṇa of Subhūticandana and the Saṃvaṇṇanānayadīpanī of Jambudhaja (Dictionary of Pāli Proper Names) remain open as before.

**Areas not covered.** The vernacular literature — Burmese nissayas and Thai and Lanna yojanā — runs to dozens of works and is represented here by one row per tradition. Besides Ñāṇatusita, the best sources for completing the list are O. H. Pind's survey of Pāli grammar and grammarians (JPTS XXXI, 2012) and A. Ruiz-Falqués's Cambridge doctoral thesis on the Suttaniddesa (2016).
