## [Saññāpanaṃ] (PDF p. 4)

Saññāpanaṃ

Padarūpasiddhiyā saṃvaṇṇanābhūtā Padarūpasiddhiṭīkā Padarūpasiddhikārakeneva Dīpaṅkarāparanāmena Buddhappiyācariyena katā, tabbhāvo ca ganthāvasāne Ganthavaṃse ca vuttavacanehi viññāyati. Tathā hi vuttaṃ ganthāvasāne “iti Bhadantabuddhappiyācariyena katā Padarūpasiddhiṭīkā samattā”ti. Ganthavaṃse ca “Rūpasiddhiganthassa ṭīkāgantho. Pe. Attano matiyā Dīpaṅkarācariyena kato”ti.

Sā ca ṭīkā ācariyena saṅkhepeneva katā hoti yaṃ yaṃ ekantena vaṇṇitabbaṃ taṃ tadeva vaṇṇayantena. Evaṃ saṅkhepakatāpi ayaṃ ṭīkā tassa tassa līnatthassa sūcanato sotūnaṃ ñāṇaṃ vibodhentī paripuṇṇāyeva atthato hoti. Tasmāyeva sā yāvajjatanāpi Padarūpasiddhiyā parivārabhāvena paṭhitā pāṭhitā ca hoti.

Yadipimissā ṭīkāya Buddhappiyācariyeneva racitabhāvo yathādassitāgamavacanehi sampaṭicchitabbo, ṭīkāyaṃ pana Padarūpasiddhiyā viruddhāni kānici ṭhānāni dissanteva. Tathā hi “tassa vā nattaṃ sabbattha”ti sutte “sabbatthā”ti padassa vaṇṇanāvasare Padarūpasiddhiyaṃ⁽¹⁾ “sabbattha liṅgesū”ti vuttaṃ Kaccāyanavuttianusārena, ṭīkāyaṃ pana taṃ aññathā vaṇṇitaṃ “sabbatthāti sabbattha vibhattīsū”ti⁽²⁾. Tathā Gāṇḍīvadhanvā-padarūpasādhane⁽³⁾ dhanusaddassa ukārassa “vamodudantānan”ti iminā va-kāro kato, ṭīkāyaṃ⁽⁴⁾ pana “yavakārā cā”ti suttena. Yaṃ pana kumbhakāra-padanipphādane ṭīkāyaṃ⁽⁵⁾ viruddhaṃ “paccayantassāliṅgattā. Pe. syādyuppattī”ti⁽⁶⁾ vacanaṃ Padarūpasiddhiyaṃ dissati, taṃ pana ṭīkāya viruddhattāyeva “pacchā kenaci pakkhittaṃ adhikavacanan”ti vadanti ācariyā. Evamādīni aññamaññaviruddhaṭṭhānāni dissanti. Tasmā upaparikkhitvāva sampaṭicchitabbā.

Ayañca ṭīkā Chaṭṭhasaṅgītisodhakamahātheravarehi Sīhaḷa-Marammamūlehi saṃsanditvā visodhitā Buddhasāsanasamitiyā Tipiṭakamuddaṇālaye suṭṭhu muddāpitāti.
