## [p. 1]

Padarūpasiddhiṭīkā

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa

### Ganthārambhavaṇṇanā

(ka) Pakaraṇārambhe ratanattayapaṇāmapubbakaṃ saññādi⟨?pubbapaṭṭhakaṃ⟩ dassetuṃ “visuddhasaddhamma”iccādinā gāthādvayaṃ āraddhaṃ. Tāsu ādigāthā vaṃsaṭṭhaṃ nāma vuttaṃ⁽¹⁾, itarā indavajirā nāma vuttaṃ⁽¹⁾. Tattha “saddhammasaṅghaṃ jinaṃ sirasā abhivandiya padarūpasiddhiṃ ujuṃ karissan”ti sambandho.

Sataṃ dhammo, santo vā pasattho, saṃvijjamāno vā dhammo saddhammo, pariyattidhammo. Saddhammo eva nānānayavicittatāya, avijjandhakāravidhamanato ca sahassadīdhiti anekasahassarasmīti saddhammasahassadīdhiti. Visuddhā nirupakkilesatāya accantaparisuddhā saddhammasaṅkhātā sahassadīdhitiyo assāti **visuddhasaddhammasahassadīdhiti**, buddhādicco, taṃ.

Suṭṭhu sammā cattāri saccāni bujjhi yathāsakaṃ pariññābhisamayādivasena paṭivijjhīti subuddhā, sāyeva pāramiparibhāvitatāya sayaṃ saccābhisambodhato sambodhi cāti

## [p. 2]

subuddhasambodhi, sabbassapi guṇassa dāyakaṃ buddhasantānasambhūtaṃ arahattamaggañāṇaṃ. Atha vā bhagavatā subuddhā suppaṭividdhā anuttaravimokkhādhigamena paṭiladdhā tāyeva sabbākārena sabbadhammābhisambodhato sambodhi cāti subuddhasambodhi, ākaṅkhapaṭibaddhavutti anāvaraṇañāṇasaṅkhātaṃ sabbaññutaññāṇaṃ. Sāyeva sāvakādibodhito, tadaññabuddhañāṇato vā visiṭṭhatāya ⟨?accuggatattena⟩, udayādhārabhāvena ca yugandharagirisadisattā yugandharo viyāti yugandharo. Atha vā sambodhiyeva sāvakānaṃ saccābhisamayahetuttā attaparasantānasaṅkhātaṃ yugaṃ yugaḷaṃ dharetīti yugandharo. Tasmiṃ yugandhare visayādhārabhūte udito kilesandhakāravidhamanādikiccabhūtaṃ sādhento maggapaṭipāṭiyā gantvā arahattamaggasaṅkhātaudayagirippattito sabbaññutaññāṇamaṇḍalobhāsobhito hutvā uggato, sabbaññutaññāṇodayagirippattito vā sabbaññubuddhabhāvena sadevake loke anuttaraṃ sammāsambodhiṃ abhisambuddho, sayaṃ ñātabhāvena vā udito pākaṭabhāvaṃ gatoti **subuddhasambodhiyugandharoditaṃ**, taṃ.

**Tibuddhakhettekadivākaran**ti tatoyeva ca divākarassa viya sakimeva tīsu dīpesu ekasseva sabbaññubuddhassa jātiāṇāñāṇānubhāvānaṃ pavattiṭṭhānatāya jātikhetta āṇākhetta visayakhettasaṅkhātesu tīsu buddhakhettesu desanāñāṇālokakaraṇena ekadivākaraṃ asādhāraṇādiccabhūtaṃ, tattha tadaññassa tādisassa sabbaññubuddhassa sahuppattiyā niratthakāditāya anavakāsattā. **Jinan**ti devaputtakilesābhisaṅkhāramaccukhandhamārasaṅkhāte pañcavidhamāre balavidhamanasamucchedapahānanidānupacchedavisayātikkamādivasena jitavāti jino, ⟨?khīṇāsavādikāya⟩ buddhasseveta nāmaṃ. Taṃ jinaṃ vijitapañcamāraṃ.

## [p. 3]

Navalokuttarasaṅkhātena paṭivedhadhammena ca aṭṭhaariyapuggalasamūhasaṅkhātena sāvakasaṅghena ca saha⁽¹⁾, sahitaṃ katvā vā tena paṭividdhabhūtadhammarūpakāyavisesattā **saddhammasaṅghaṃ** jinaṃ buddhādiccaṃ **sirasā** uttamaṅgena **abhivandiya** abhivanditvā, tīhi dvārehi sakkaccaṃ namassitvāti attho.

Tattha “jinan”ti iminā satthu pubbacariyaparakkamavijayappahānapūjārahatādisampadā dassitā, yā “attuddhāro”tipi vuccati. “Visuddhasaddhammasahassadīdhitin”ti iminā desanādilakkhaṇaparahitapaṭipattisampadā dassitā, yā “lokuddhāro”tipi vuccati. “Subuddhasambodhiyugandharoditan”ti vacanena anāvaraṇañāṇādhigamasampadā dassitā, dasabalādiguṇānubhāvapaṭilābhasampadā ca dassitā, yā “dhammañāṇuddhāro”tipi vuccati. “Tibuddhakhettekadivākaran”ti iminā jātiāṇāñāṇavisayadesanādiānubhāvasampadā, asadisatā ca dassitā. “Saddhammasaṅghan”ti iminā dhammarūpakāyaparivārasampadā dassitā. “Sirasābhivandiyā”ti iminā yathāvuttaguṇavisiṭṭhe pūjārahe satthari kāyavācācittehi satthuguṇaninna⟨?tā⟩lakkhaṇo abhippetatthasādhako paṇāmo dassitoti daṭṭhabbaṃ.

(kha) Evaṃ pakaraṇārambhe maṅgalādiatthaṃ thutippaṇāmehi ratanattayabhūtassa pūjanīyassa satthussa sādaraṃ pūjaṃ vidhāya idāni ārabhitumicchitassa pakaraṇassa nissayaganthakattuno paṇāmaṃ, pakaraṇanissayapayojanābhidhānābhidheyyakaraṇappakāre ca dassetuṃ “Kaccāyanañcā”tiādi vuttaṃ. Tattha **Kaccāyanañcā**ti bhagavatā saṃkhittena bhāsitassa vitthārena atthaṃ vibhajantānaṃ sāvakānaṃ etadagge ṭhapitaṃ mahāsāvakaṃ niruttilakkhaṇācariyabhūtaṃ mahākaccāyanattherañca namitvā namassitvā kaccāyanabyākaraṇañca taṃvaṇṇanābhūtaṃ

ñāsañca, **ādi**saddena sandhiniruttippakaraṇādikañca nissāya nissayaṃ katvā **bālappabodhanatthaṃ** bālānaṃ saddalakkhaṇānabhiññānaṃ tadavabodhanatthaṃ **byattaṃ** lakkhaṇavisayavavatthānassa, lakkhiyābhinibbattiyā ca paribyattito abhibyattaṃ supākaṭaṃ sandhiliṅgādīsu kalāpatāya **sukaṇḍaṃ** suparicchedaṃ ujuṃ lakkhaṇānukkamaṃ vihāya lakkhiyapadarūpasiddhianukkamakathitatāya ujukaṃ **padarūpasiddhiṃ** nāmākhyātopasagganipātasaṅkhātānaṃ catubbidhānaṃ padānaṃ vibhattyantarūpanipphattisādhanatāya padarūpasiddhisaññitaṃ pakaraṇaṃ **karissaṃ** karissāmīti attho.

## [p. 5]

### 1. Sandhikaṇḍa

#### Saddalakkhaṇārambhavaṇṇanā

Idāni saddalakkhaṇārambhe taṃsavanānisaṃsadassanatthaṃ ācariyena “attho akkharasaññāto”ti vākyamidamāraddhanti dassetuṃ “tattha jinasāsanādhigamassā”tiādi vuttaṃ. **Tatthā**ti tissaṃ padarūpasiddhiyaṃ abhidheyyappayojanavākyamidamuccateti sambandho. Jinassa sāsanaṃ jinasāsanaṃ, taṃ pana tividhaṃ pariyattipaṭipattipaṭivedhasāsanavasena. Tassa tividhassa jinasāsanassa adhigamo ⟨?avabodho⟩ jinasāsanādhigamo, tassa. **Akkharakosallamūlakattā**⁽¹⁾ti akkharesu saravyañjanādibhedesu, akkharasamudāyesu nāmikādipadesu ca yaṃ kosallaṃ kusalatā paṇḍiccaṃ, taṃmūlakattā. Tathā hi akkharakosalle sati tipiṭakasaṅkhātassa sāṭṭhakathassa pariyattidhammassādhigamo⁽²⁾ hoti, tasmiṃ sati sikkhattayasaṅkhātassa paṭipattidhammassādhigamo hoti, tasmiñca sati navalokuttaradhammasaṅkhātassa paṭivedhadhammassādhigamo hoti, na vinā tenānukkamena. Tasmā taṃ akkharakosallaṃ sampādetabbanti **dassetuṃ** dassanatthaṃ. **Abhidheyyappayojanavākyaṃ** abhidheyyassa akkharasaṅkhātassa saddānusāsanābhidheyyassa byākaraṇasatthassa⁽³⁾ yaṃ payojanaṃ vacanatthāvabodhanalakkhaṇaṃ phalaṃ, tassa paridīpakaṃ vākyaṃ aññamaññasambandhaṃ ekatthapaṭipādakapadasamudāyarūpaṃ “attho akkharasaññāto”ti idamādivākyamuccateti attho. **Abhidheyyappayojanakathanañcettha** pakaraṇassārambhanīyatādassanatthaṃ. Satipi sakatthapaṭipādakalakkhaṇe

## [p. 6]

pakaraṇappayojane abhidheyyappayojanābhāve vitaṇḍāsatthaṃ viya anārambhanīyatāti.

1. Tattha ariyati ñāyatīti **attho**. Akkharehi sammā ñāyatīti **akkharasaññāto**. “Attho”ti avisesena vuttattā, vuttiyañca “sabbavacanānamattho”ti vacanatthavasena vuttattā “yo koci vacanattho”ti sabbasaṅgāhakavasena vuttaṃ. Evaṃ anavasesavacanatthānuvādena appasiddhassa akkharasaññāpanattassa vidhānaṃ vuttaṃ, na tu ñāṇindriyattādikassa. Loke viditoti **lokiyo**. So pana tebhūmakavaṭṭapariyāpanno kusalādikhandhāyatanādibhedabhinno sāsavadhammo. **Lokuttaro** nāma maggaphalanibbānasaṅkhāto navavidho anāsavadhammo. **Ādisaddena** sammutisantānādibhedabhinno **vohārattho** gayhati.

Sithiladhanitādīsu **sithilaṃ** nāma vaggesu paṭhamatatiyā, **dhanitaṃ** nāma dutiyacatutthā. **Ādisaddena** dīgharassagarukalahukādi gayhati. **Akkharavipatti** nāma sithilādīnaṃ dhanitādibhāvena aññathattagamanaṃ, yathā “akkharavipattiyan”ti vattabbe “akkharavibhattiyan”ti vacanaṃ. Evaṃ hi sati vacanatthassa **dunnayatā** dunnīyabhāvo viparītena gayhamānatā hoti, tasmā akkharānaṃ sarūpādijānanaṃ icchitabbaṃ. Tena vuttaṃ “akkhareheva saññāyate”ti sāvadhāraṇaṃ. Akkharāni hi ñāpakahetuttā viññātasarūpasambandhāni eva vacanatthaṃ sammā ñāpenti, yathā dhūmādikaṃ liṅgaṃ aggiādikamanumeyyatthamiti. **Yasmā**ti ādivākye. **Tasmā** kāraṇā **hitatthiko** sāsanādhigamalakkhaṇena hitena atthiko taṃ hitaṃ patthayamāno puggalo **uṭṭhānādīhi** uṭṭhānaupaṭṭhānasussūsāpāricariyāsakkaccasippuggahaṇasaṅkhātehi pañcahi kāraṇehi garuṃ ācariyaṃ sammā **upaṭṭhahaṃ** sakkaccaṃ

## [p. 7]

upaṭṭhahanto **akkharakosallaṃ** akkharesu paṭutarañāṇasaṅkhātaṃ kosallaṃ **sampādeyya** nipphādeyyāti attho.

#### Saññāvidhānavaṇṇanā

Tattha tasmiṃ pakaraṇe **ādo** ādimhi sandhipadese tāva paṭhamaṃ saññāvidhānamārabhīyateti sambandho. Kimatthanti ce? Ye saddalakkhaṇe kaccāyanabyākaraṇasutte sarabyañjanaghosādikā akkharavohārā dissanti, tesaṃ viññāpanatthaṃ, aviññātasaññassa vohārāsijjhanatoti.

2. “Sambandho ca padañceva, padattho padaviggaho.
Codanā parihāro ca, chabbidhā suttavaṇṇanā”ti⁽¹⁾

ñāpakato⁽²⁾ suttaṃ vaṇṇetukāmena suttassa sambandhādikaṃ vattabbaṃ. Tattha sutte pubbāparapadānaṃ ekavākyatāya yojanaṃ **sambandho**, ⟨?imassa⟩ saddalakkhaṇe suttassa sātthakatādassanena vā sambandhadassanaṃ veditabbaṃ. Sutte padacchedavasena **padaṃ** veditabbaṃ. Suttatthavasena **padattho** veditabbo. Sutte vijjamānasamāsataddhitakitakapadānaṃ samāsādiviggahavākyadassanaṃ **viggaho**. Sutte padānaṃ payojanādipucchanaṃ **codanā**. Sutte padānaṃ sātthakatādidassanavasena parihāraṇaṃ **parihāro**. Tattha “akkharāpādayo ekacattālīsan”ti akkharasaññāvidhāyakamidaṃ suttan”ti vutte suttasambandho, padasambandho ca dassitova hoti, akkharasaññāya saddalakkhaṇopayogato, akkharādipadānaṃ aññamaññasambandhavasena tadekatthābhidhānavākyena “dasa dāḍimādīnī”⁽³⁾ viya asambandhavākyabhāvanivattito ca.

## [p. 8]

Tattha “akkharā”ti saññāniddeso. “Apī”ti samuccayaniddeso. “Ādayo”ti saññiniddeso. “Ekacattālīsan”ti saññivisesanaṃ. “Paccekaṃ akkharā nāma hontī”ti iminā avayave vākyaparisamattīti dassitaṃ hoti, na samudāye, yathā “gaggakulassa sataṃ daṇḍetī”ti⁽¹⁾. **Taṃ yathā**ti te katameti sāmaññena vuttānaṃ sarūpapucchā. **Iti akkharā**ti evaṃ iminānukkamena vuttā akārādayo ekacattālīsamattā ime akkharā nāmāti attho.

“Nakkharantī”ti iminā samāsakitakaviggahavasena padaviggaho dassito. Tattha kharanti vinassantīti kharā, saṅkhatā. “Nakkharantī”ti paṭikkhepena “akkharā”ti akārādināmapaññatti dassitā. Sā hi paramatthato anipphannattā na nassati. Vuttañhi “nāmagottaṃ na jīratī”ti⁽²⁾. “Akkharā”ti garusaññākaraṇampi anvatthasaññāvasena akārādināmapaññattiyā akkharasabhāvadassanatthameva. “A ādi yesaṃ te **ādayo**”ti iminā tagguṇasaṃviññāṇabahubbīhi dassito. **Ādisaddo** panettha pakārattho⁽³⁾. Akārādīnaṃ pana so yathāvutto anukkamo na uppattikkamasiddho, nāpi akārasseva padhānattā, api tu kaṇṭhādiṭṭhānapaṭipāṭiṃ, nissayādikkamañca nissāyāti dassetuṃ “akārādīnamanukkamo”tiādi vuttaṃ. “Ṭhānādikkamasaṇṭhito”ti ettha **ādisaddena** nissayādikaraṇaṃ gayhati. Sāmaññena vuttamatthaṃ visesena dassetuṃ “tathā hī”tiādi vuttaṃ. Tiṭṭhanti ettha vaṇṇā tadāyattavuttitāyāti ṭhānaṃ, kaṇṭhādi. Kariyante uccāriyante etenāti karaṇaṃ, jivhāmajjhādi. **Payatanaṃ** pana yathārahaṃ ṭhānakaraṇānaṃ saṃvutādiguṇo.

## [p. 9]

Tattha ṭhānādīsu **avaṇṇo** ca kavaggo ca hakāro cāti ime vaṇṇā kaṇṭhe jāyantīti **kaṇṭhajā**. Yadi nāma cittajo vacīghoso, tathāpi kaṇṭhādīsu tassa abhibyatti hotīti. **Tālu** nāma jivhāmajjhena yasmiṃ ṭhāne ghaṭṭite ivaṇṇādayo jāyanti, taṃ ṭhānaṃ. **Ṅa ña ṇa na mā** pana anunāsikattā sakaṭṭhāne ca nāsikaṭṭhāne ca jāyanti.

Hakāraṃ panettha “brahmacariyan”tiādīsu viya vaggapañcamehi ca, pañcavaggānamante ṭhitattā antaṭṭhanāmakehi **saṃyuttaṃ** sahayuttaṃ, sahakāraṃ vā “orasaṃ urasi bhavatī”ti vadanti. **Tabbisaṃyuttan**ti tehi asaṃyuttaṃ “kaṇṭhajan”ti vadantīti attho.

Aggassa upa samīpaṃ upaggaṃ, jivhāya upaggaṃ **jivhopaggaṃ**. Tena jivhāpadesena iti vuccati. **Dantesu** jāyantīti **dantajā**. Sesā kavaggapavaggādayo. Tathā hi jivhāghaṭṭanaṃ vinā sakaṭṭhānaphusaneneva “pakati, itī”evamādinā vuccati.

**Saṃvutattan**ti avivaṭattaṃ. Kaṇṭhajasaresupi ekārokāratopi **ākārassa** vivaṭattaṃ payatanaṃ.

**Sutibhedo** ghosādi. **Kālabhedo** ekamattādi. Byañjanehi nissīyantīti **nissayā**. Sarā **ādo** ādimhiyeva padhānabhāvena vuttā. Tathā hi “nissayanissitesu nissayāva paṭhamaṃ vattabbā”ti sarāva paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Tato pacchā pana nissitā byañjanā vuttā. Vaggā ca ekajā ca bahukattā ādo vuttā. Tattha saresu ye ekajā, te bahukā, dvijā appakāti “appakabahukesu bahukāva paṭhamaṃ vattabbā”ti bahukattā ekajā avaṇṇādayo paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Puna kaṇṭhatāluoṭṭhaṭṭhānānukkamena vuttā. Tato appakattā dvijā ekārokārā pacchā ṭhānānukkamena vuttā. Ekajesupi “garukalahukesu lahukāva paṭhamaṃ

## [p. 10]

vattabbā”ti parittato lahukāva paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Byañjanesupi vaggā vaggānaṃ bahukattā vaggāva paṭhamaṃ kaṇṭhādiṭṭhānapaṭipāṭiyā vuttā. Tatthāpi appakattā dviṭhānā pañcamā pacchā vuttā. Lahukatarattā vaggesu aghosāva paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Tato garukānīti. Avaggesupi bahukattā ghosabhūtā yaralavā ṭhānānukkamena paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Sakāro aghosattā, appakattā ca tato pacchā vutto. Hakāro pana kaṇṭhajattā, ghosattā ca paṭhamaṃ vattabbopi ⟨?upaṭṭhipāḷiyāpi⟩ akkharuppattisambhavadassanatthaṃ pacchā vutto. Ḷakāraṃ pana keci ḍakāraṭṭhāne paṭhanti, ⟨?ima⟩ ḷakāraṭṭhāneti dassanatthaṃ so pacchā vutto. Niggahītaṃ pana assaratā, avaggatā, ghosāghosavinimuttatā ca sabbapacchā vuttanti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Ayaṃ panānukkamo ñāse vuttoti dassanatthaṃ “pubbaṃ pana ṭhānānan”tiādinā saṅgahagāthā dassitā.

“Ekacattālīsan”ti paṭhamekavacanantapadaṃ. **Ācariyāsabho** jeṭṭhausabhasadiso ācariyo āgamādhigamasampanno mahākaccāyanatthero. “Ekacattālīsan”ti iminā suttantesu pakārānamakkharānaṃ gaṇanaparicchedavacanena **ito** yathāvuttaekacattālīsamattato **adhikakkharavantāni** sakkatagandhesu upalabbhamānehi adhikakkharehi vihitāni yāni yāni vacanāni, tāni na buddhavacanānīti ñāpetīti attho.

**⟨?Tehā⟩ vuttānan**ti “attho akkharasaññāto”ti ettha vuttānamakkharānanti attho.

3. **Tatthā**ti niddhāraṇe sattamī, “tvādayo vibhattisaññāyo”ti vibhattisaññāya vuttattā, “dakāro sandhijo”ti sithilaniddesattaṃ sandhivasena vutto. “Antā”ti gaṇanaparicchedo vā bahiddhā avadhikāpi santīti ñāpanatthaṃ.

## [p. 11]

4. **Accharāsaṅghāto** nāma aṅguliphoṭanaṃ. **Mattā**ti gāthāvisaye garulahuvicāre. Byañjanasahitatāya ⟨?diyaḍḍhamattopi⟩ rasso “lahuko”ti gayhati. Tassāpi gahaṇatthaṃ “ekamattā”ti avatvā “lahumattā”ti ñāse⁽¹⁾ vuttaṃ. Sithiladhanitādīhi dasavidhabyañjanavicāre saṃyogapubbo rassopi “garuko”ti gayhati. Idha pana kālaniyamassa avuttatāya dīghakālapaṭikkhepena lahukālayogaṃ upādāya anvatthavasena “lahumattā”ti vuttanti daṭṭhabbaṃ. **Rassakālavantatā vā**ti rassasaṅkhāto kālo etesanti **rassā**. “Saddhādito ṇa”iti ṇapaccayo.
